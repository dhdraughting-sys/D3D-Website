MEP Quick Calcs

The same formulas behind MEP Design Platform, free to use for a quick indicative mechanical & plumbing design check.

Please note: all calculations follow relevant CIBSE / IHVE / Building Regulations methodology, but are provided for indicative purposes only. Always have a qualified engineer verify figures before use on a live project — this tool is not a substitute for a full design check.

Cold Water Storage

Storage volume and Legionella turnover check, using the CIBSE/IHVE Plumbing Engineering Services Design Guide method.

Hot Water Storage

Quick sizing for hot water storage vessels against demand.

Pipe / Duct Sizing

Velocity and pressure-drop-checked pipe and duct sizing for a given flow rate.

Condensation Checks

Pipe insulation, duct insulation and room surface condensation risk, against ambient conditions.

Summer Overheating Quick Check

Indicative overheating risk score for a room, in the spirit of CIBSE TM59 / Part O — glazing, orientation, shading, fabric, ventilation and gains.
